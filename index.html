About the Overwatch rank distribution chart

This interactive chart shows how Overwatch competitive players are spread across the ranks, from Bronze to Champion. Move across the bell curve to see which rank sits at each SR and percentile, what share of players is in each tier and division, and how far it is to the next division. Use the finder to jump to an SR, a percentage of players to beat, or a rank, and pin one rank to compare it with another.

The curve for each season is fitted to community-submitted datapoints, each checked against a screenshot, so every figure is an estimate rather than an official Blizzard statistic.
